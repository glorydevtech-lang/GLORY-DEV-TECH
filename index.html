About

GLORY DEV TECH is run by Harish Gurjar, a self-taught Android developer based in Ramganj Mandi, Kota, Rajasthan. The studio designs, builds, and maintains every app in its lineup end-to-end — from Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on the front end through to Play Console publishing and compliance.

The goal is simple: small, well-built utility and learning apps that solve one problem cleanly, published under a single accountable name rather than an anonymous team.
